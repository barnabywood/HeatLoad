How to track calories burned in a sauna on Apple Watch.

What Apple Watch can show, what Sauna Log records, and why calorie values can vary between sessions.

ANSWER

Does Apple Watch track calories in a sauna?

Apple Watch can provide active and total calorie values during a Sauna Log session when the required health data is available and permission has been granted.

ANSWER

Are sauna calorie estimates exact?

No. Calorie values are estimates based on the information Apple Watch and Apple Health provide. They can vary with heart rate, movement, fit, session length and the data available.

ANSWER

How does Sauna Log save calorie data?

Start the session on Apple Watch and Sauna Log displays available active and total calorie values. The completed record keeps those values and syncs to iPhone when the devices reconnect.

ANSWER

Does Sauna Log write to Apple Health?

With permission, Sauna Log writes eligible workout and heart-rate data through HealthKit. Apple may display the workout under a generic category rather than a dedicated sauna category.
