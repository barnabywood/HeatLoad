WATCH INSTALLATION

New Watch?

Install Sauna Log on the paired iPhone, then install the companion on Apple Watch or Garmin. Keep the phone and watch nearby while pairing and installing.

HEALTH ACCESS

Missing heart rate?

Open Health permissions from Sauna Log settings and allow the requested heart-rate and workout access. A snug Watch fit improves readings.

PURCHASES

Unlock not showing?

Open Support in the iPhone app and use Restore Purchase or Sync Unlock to Watch. If the Watch says Unlock on iPhone, refresh the state from the phone.
