Questions, answered.

Clear answers for people deciding whether Sauna Log is right for their sauna routine.

Is Sauna Log a sauna timer for Apple Watch and Garmin?

Yes. Sauna Log is designed to run the timer and session tracking from a compatible Apple Watch or Garmin watch, with the iPhone app used for settings, history, purchases and insights.

Does Sauna Log support Garmin?

Sauna Log adds a Garmin companion alongside Apple Watch. Garmin users can start and monitor a heat timer from a compatible Garmin watch, while the iPhone app manages settings and history.

Can I track a steam room session?

Yes. Choose Sauna or Steam Room before starting. Each heat type can remember its own temperature and humidity defaults, and you can record conditions in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

What does Sauna Log cost?

Record three sessions free, then unlock unlimited sessions with a one-time in-app purchase. No subscription. See the current price in the iPhone app.

Does Sauna Log write to Apple Health?

With permission, Sauna Log writes eligible workout and heart-rate data through HealthKit. The selected heat type is stored in Sauna Log metadata; Apple may display the workout under its generic category.

Can I use it away from my iPhone?

Yes. The Watch app is designed to operate during a session without your phone nearby, then sync the completed record when the devices reconnect.
